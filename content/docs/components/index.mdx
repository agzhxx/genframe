So far: [CompareSlider](./compare-slider.mdx), a before/after slider for two images or two
videos; [AspectRatioPicker](./aspect-ratio-picker.mdx); [ModelPicker](./model-picker.mdx), with an
estimate of what each model would cost; [PromptComposer](./prompt-composer.mdx), with @-mentions
of reference images; [GenerationQueue](./generation-queue.mdx), with its
[cards](./generation-card.mdx) and the [denoise reveal](./denoise-reveal.mdx) of their results; and
[MediaGrid](./media-grid.mdx), a masonry grid of [media cards](./media-card.mdx) whose clips preview
from a shared pool of videos; [VideoScrubber](./video-scrubber.mdx), a scrub bar with thumbnail
previews and frame steps; and [CameraMotionPicker](./camera-motion-picker.mdx), whose tiles play
their camera moves from the same pool.

## Every component page has

- A **playground** with the component running live, its controls, and two toggles. **Slow
  motion (0.25×)** plays every animation at a quarter of its speed; **Reduced motion** switches
  the component to its reduced version.
- **Installation**: the command, with the namespace or by URL.
- **Usage**: an example to start from.
- **Props**: read from the component's TypeScript types and comments.
- **Keyboard**: every key it handles. The table and the component's tests read the same file,
  so what's published is what's tested.
- **Accessibility**: its WAI-ARIA pattern, its labels and what screen readers announce.
- **Motion**: what moves and why, and what the reduced version does instead.
- **Changelog**: the releases that changed it.

The size next to each title is the component's own code plus the npm packages it brings,
minified and gzipped ([Sizes](../index.mdx#sizes)).
